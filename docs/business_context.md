# Project Overview & Business Context

## Situation

Cross-border payments generate large volumes of structured payment activity. Investigation teams need more than a transaction score: they need relevant transaction, relationship, behavioral and institutional context that can be reviewed consistently.

## Problem

A score-only monitoring view can leave investigators with a high-volume queue and insufficiently organized supporting evidence.

## Question

Can structured cross-border payment information be turned into a governed investigation workflow that links risk signal, contextual evidence and investigator review?

## Answer

This prototype demonstrates such a workflow using a synthetic SWIFT payment environment. The design connects payment records, payment relationships, a frozen predictive model, canonical evidence retrieval, investigation cases, and downstream AI-assisted case analysis.

## Business outcome demonstrated

The project creates a traceable chain from a payment event to a reviewable investigation package rather than stopping at a model score.

## Operational users

- Risk leadership
- AML operations management
- AML investigators
- Compliance and model-risk teams
- Data science teams
- AI and engineering teams
- Analytics / consulting reviewers
