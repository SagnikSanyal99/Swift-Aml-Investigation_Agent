# Audience Guide

## Executive / Risk Leadership

Lead with scale, investigation coverage, governance, and the end-to-end workflow.

Recommended figures:

- 100,000 payment edges
- 36,979 accounts
- 0.972667 ROC-AUC
- 30,000 evidence records
- 1,000 selected investigations

## AML Operations

Focus on queue volume, case mix, evidence availability, and investigation workflow.

## AML Investigator

Focus on case selection, model signal, transaction context, network context, behavioral context, and evidence traceability.

## Compliance / Model Risk

Focus on temporal separation, immutability, checksum verification, ground-truth isolation, and known limitations.

## Data Science / ML

Focus on graph scale, reproducible metrics, controlled experiments, and the topology contribution.

## AI / Engineering

Focus on the evidence store, traceability, successful AI investigations, and system boundaries.

## Recruiter / Interviewer

Focus on the combination of banking domain knowledge, analytics, machine learning, evidence governance, and AI-assisted workflow design.
