# Presentation Sequence

## 8-minute executive demonstration

### 1. Open with the bottom line

State the one-line project claim and show the executive KPI panel.

### 2. Explain the business problem

Use the Situation → Problem → Question → Answer narrative from `docs/business_context.md`.

### 3. Show the operating structure

Walk from payment message to entity, relationship, contextual information, risk signal, evidence, and investigation case.

### 4. Show the model result

Present the frozen model metrics, then immediately show the controlled experiment rather than presenting the model as an isolated score.

### 5. Show investigator value

Open the investigation view and demonstrate how model signal is paired with transaction, network and behavioral evidence.

### 6. Show governance

Show the train/test split, frozen checkpoint, contamination result, evidence citation result, and limitations.

### 7. Show AI assistance

Explain that the AI component operates downstream of the evidence layer and was validated on 32 persisted successful investigations.

### 8. Close with the complete workflow

**Payment data → Payment relationships → Risk signal → Evidence → Investigation → AI assistance → Audit trail**

## Audience adaptation

For executives: lead with scale, outcomes and governance.

For AML investigators: lead with case workflow and evidence.

For model-risk teams: lead with validation, immutability and isolation.

For data-science teams: lead with graph scale and controlled experiments.

For recruiters: lead with the cross-functional combination of banking, analytics, machine learning, governance and AI-assisted workflow design.
